# Signova V2 - Word video recording kit

This kit requests **256 new original recordings across all 20 supported words**. Open each word folder to see its sign-reference link, suggested number of clips, current training and test counts, and exact filenames. `recording-plan.csv` is the complete checklist.

## Recording checklist

1. Open the word's `SIGN_GUIDE.url` lesson as a reference. It is an ASL teaching page; the linked video is not a Signova training clip.
2. Record yourself performing only that word, one sign per 2-5 second video. Keep your face, shoulders, arms, and hands visible.
3. Save MP4 clips in that word's `recordings/` folder with the numbered names in its README.
4. Vary lighting and distance a little across recordings; do not film another screen or download the reference lesson.
5. Zip the filled `word-recording-kit` folder and send it back. Keep the old test data untouched; I will integrate the new recordings into training only after checking labels, format, duplicates, and split placement.

## Priority

- **P1:** 13 low-scoring or low-support classes; record 20 for drink and 15 for each of the other P1 words.
- **P2:** book, deaf, eat, want, water; record 8 each.
- **P3:** family and school; record 8 each if time allows.

The existing per-word test groups are small (1-8 clips), so 0% or 100% in this report is not a precise estimate of real-world accuracy. More clips can help the model learn, but they cannot guarantee 100% predictions.
