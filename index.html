THE IDEA BEHIND THE INTERFACE

About Signova

Technology should bring people closer. Signova is a student project exploring sign-language recognition.

SO
OUR MISSION

Making communication
feel more connected.

Signova's goal is to recognize selected American Sign Language (ASL) signs and present them as readable text. The frontend sends video to a local FastAPI backend that uses the trained model and optional Gemini verification.

HOW IT WORKS

Three parts. One experience.

01

Capture

The browser asks for camera permission and shows the live video preview.

FRONTEND
02

Recognize

The backend extracts pose landmarks and runs the trained 166-class model; Gemini video verification is optional.

BACKEND + MODEL
03

Communicate

The interface displays the returned word and confidence and keeps a session-only sentence history.
