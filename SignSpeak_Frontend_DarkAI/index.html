THE IDEA BEHIND THE INTERFACE

About SignSpeak

Technology should bring people closer. SignSpeak is a student project exploring sign-language recognition.

SS
OUR MISSION

Making communication
feel more connected.

SignSpeak's goal is to recognize selected American Sign Language (ASL) signs and present them as readable text. The current interface is a frontend prototype; real recognition depends on your team's model and backend.
